Self-regulated learning across family, school, and community

This graphic maps how family, school, and community support or limit a student's capacity for self-regulated learning with technology. It adapts Bronfenbrenner's (1979) ecological systems theory and treats the K–12 school career as a fixed timeline. Select a stage to see which systems have the most influence and what helps or gets in the way at that point. After graduation, the learner gives back to the community as a contributor through technology.

Note. Influence levels and the listed supports and barriers are working hypotheses for further research, not findings. Concept by Anthony White. Layout and interactive code developed with Claude (Anthropic, 2026). Illustrations generated with Gemini (Google, 2026).
